BEHIND THE WORK

Turning gameplay into stories

A closer look at the thinking, process and outcome.

Purpose

Create video content and a trailer that introduce the possibilities of Newton’s Playground to potential players.

Challenge

Communicate the appeal of a VR sandbox: the experience is built around experimentation, so the content needs to show what players can actually do.

Process

I wrote scripts for short- and long-form content, turned mechanics and creative possibilities into approachable stories, and produced videos for TikTok and YouTube. The project also included trailer content.

Tools

Scriptwriting, video production and platform publishing; the specific software can be confirmed during review.

Results

The existing project figures report 200K TikTok views, 100K+ YouTube views and 1K+ followers.
